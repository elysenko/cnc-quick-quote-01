What we check

  • File type and size against your workshop's upload limits.
  • True arc length for curved segments, so curves are not under-priced.
  • Overall bounding box, used to nest parts on the sheet.
  • Anything we cannot read is reported, never silently ignored.

Malformed or empty files are rejected outright. A valid but messy drawing (open shapes, stray construction lines) will still quote — check the preview on the next step.
